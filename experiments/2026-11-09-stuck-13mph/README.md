- Question and expected observation: the user reported the vehicle getting stuck at "12–13 mph" with the throttle held. A background research agent (`ShiftLogicResearch`) enumerated the mechanism from code and telemetry: at ~7.5 m/s (12–13 mph while steering) the automatic gearbox sits exactly on its 1↔2 boundary. The upshift guard demands engine rpm > 6200 **and** `coupledRpm > 5800`; the downshift guard fires when `coupledRpm < 2300` with the lower-gear re-expression < 5580 (`src/simulation.cpp` shift evaluator). Near the boundary, small speed dips make the box downshift; the shift opens the clutch, the engine free-revs to the redline, the upshift guard then passes, the box upshifts again — every ~100–200 ms. Each shift forces `shiftRemaining_ = 0.22 s` of zero clutch engagement, so the wheels get no power; speed stagnates at the boundary: rpm pegged at redline, clutch ~0, speed pinned — exactly the telemetry (gear flickering 1↔2 at ~7.5 m/s, clutch 0.00, accel ~0, for 14 s+).

- Observation: the headless full-throttle probes did not catch the bug because flat-out runs blow through the boundary band instead of hovering in it; the live trigger involved steering drag holding the car in the band. The user's fix redesigns the shift governor:
  1. Road-coupled shift thresholds: gear speed is estimated from chassis motion at the wheel contact points (never wheel rpm), gated on grounded supported drive weight ≥ 0.75 and normal load > 5 % of mass·g — a spinning or unloaded wheel cannot request a shift.
  2. Confirmed shift requests: a candidate must persist `shiftConfirmTime` (0.06 s) before executing.
  3. Minimum gear hold: after a shift plus clutch re-engagement, `minimumGearHoldTime` (0.55 s) blocks opposing shifts — kills the flap.
  4. Re-engagement ramp: clutch engagement and throttle torque ramp back over `shiftReengagementTime` (0.12 s) instead of snapping.
  5. Ratio-aware downshift hysteresis with over-rev guards on both wheel speeds.
  6. Throttle-dependent upshift point (`partThrottleShiftUpRpm` 2800 blending to `shiftUpRpm` 6200 with pedal) — also closes the "light throttle holds gear 1 to redline" open item.
  7. New validation bounds in `VehicleSetup`, drivetrain diagnostics on the published `Car` (gearbox input/road-coupled/clutch-slip rpm, shift timers, `shifting`), new `drive_sample` channels, and a focused `tests/transmission_checks.cpp` suite (wheelspin/no-support cannot shift, full-throttle progression, low-grip/grade no-hunt, partial-throttle no-bog, corner recovery, stop-and-relaunch, dt invariance, finite state).
