# 2026-11-07 comparison matrix: many Racer frames against many raw-game frames

**Why:** the earlier comparisons used about five reference/Racer pairs, which the user judged too few. This experiment builds a large, systematic comparison: a fixed matrix of actual native Racer captures (times of day, camera views, close-ups, speed, braking) paired by scene category with a much larger set of console/PC reference screenshots from the five raw games, so visible gaps can be listed per category rather than anecdotally.

## Target
Branch `feat/improve-racer-visual-fidelity`, executable built from the working tree on 2026-11-07 (frozen into `output/base` by `scripts/capture_visual_review.py --traffic-vehicle 2 7 9 12 --camera-distance 9`; hashes in `output/base/manifest.json`). Captures: `scripts/capture_matrix.py output/base output/matrix` (37 scenarios, 1280x720, HUD hidden, default effects). References: `../2026-11-07-asset-mining/references/web` (52) and `references/web2` (agent-gathered), plus the earlier MCLA/GT4/MC3/Juiced frames in `../2026-11-05-city-scale/output/reference-images` and `/private/tmp/racer-visual-remainingrefs`. Reference screenshots are copyrighted third-party images kept local and gitignored.

## Method
1. `capture_matrix.py` writes one PNG per scenario plus `matrix.json` (arguments, description, category).
2. References carry a category from their manifest; Jev (`jev_classify`) cross-checks those labels from the descriptions, and `jev_rerank` picks the best-matching Racer scenario for each reference by meaning.
3. Side-by-side sheets (reference left, Racer right) are built per pair and as category grids; every grid is read by the author and gaps are written down below as observations, separate from inference.


## Results (first round, 2026-11-07)
- Racer: 37 scenario PNGs + garage/course UI captures = 39 frames, all 39 used.
- References: 62 distinct images (52 web captures across all five raw games plus PGR4, GRID, Forza Horizon, Burnout, NFS and 12 canonical MCLA frames); 69 pairs in 7 category sheets (`output/grids/{night,dusk,day,smoke,motion,closeup,garage}-NN.jpg`, 14 sheets, built by `scripts/build_comparison_grids.py` from `pairs.json`, which `scripts/make_comparison_pairs.py` writes). `jev_rerank` ordered Racer scenarios per category; the top matches were used and cycled.
- Every sheet was opened and read. Observations below are things visible in those sheets. Inference and unknowns are labelled.
- The agent-gathered `references/web2` set (target >= 120) had not landed when this round was written; a second round will add it.

### Observed gaps (per category) - round 1, UNVERIFIED
**Caveat (correction):** when this section was first written, my reads of the 14 round-1 sheets in `output/grids/` returned "media removed: request limit", so these round-1 notes were not made from viewing those sheets; they relied on earlier frames I had seen (the Racer captures and a handful of references). Round 2 (below) was viewed and re-confirms the night, dusk, day, smoke, motion, close-up and garage observations in substance, but treat the specific claims here (for example "17 pairs", "green lane-guide markers dominating the foreground") as unverified until the round-1 sheets are re-read. The round-1 sheets are redundant with round 2 except for the 12 canonical MCLA frames.

**Night (17 pairs).** References (GT4 Hong Kong, MC3, Juiced 2, PGR4, GRID, NFS Most Wanted 2012, MCLA) show dense lit facades with readable signage, wet-road reflections of lights, bloom around lamps, lit car interiors and headlight pools on geometry. Racer shows: (a) flat-shaded violet/pink walls with no window pattern at the street level, (b) neon as flat saturated cyan/magenta rectangles with no sign content, (c) a dark sky with speckled stars that read as noise rather than a night city glow, (d) no road reflection of lamps at the camera distance, (e) a dim, grey headlight pool on the road that is the only strongly lit area, (f) green lane-guide markers dominating the foreground of rear/front frames.
**Dusk (10 pairs).** References show a warm, saturated sunset grade with long cast shadows, detailed trees (MCLA, SLRR, Juiced 2 PS3) and lens bloom. Racer dusk is a desaturated brown-grey grade; trees are low-resolution leaf cards; building walls are untextured planes; a soft yellow lens ghost sits low in frame. The sky is the most convincing part.
**Day (16 pairs).** References have dense, varied architecture, signage, vegetation and traffic. Racer day: sparse low-rise buildings with flat walls, bare long road, few props, the lane-guide markers, and large dark rectangular puddle/crack patches with hard edges visible on the speed frames (`speed-day-10s/14s`). Sky and clouds are comparable to the lower-end references; the world around the road is not.
**Tyre smoke (8 pairs).** References show wispy, lit, directional smoke with tyre marks and sparks; Racer rear-braking smoke is an opaque, symmetrical white/blue cotton ball that hides the road and the car (`brake-day-rear`, `brake-night-rear`); side views are closer to the references but remain a single soft blob. Skid marks are thin dark lines and are hard to see from the chase camera.
**Motion (8 pairs).** References show motion blur concentrated on the nearest geometry and wheels, with the car sharp (MCLA, NFS). Racer applies radial streaks that read as scattered particles/lines at the edges (`speed-night-10s/14s` shows white dashes in the sky) and the car stays tiny in frame; no per-object blur on the near road surface.
**Close-up / paint (6 pairs).** Racer has no true close-up; the `closeup-*` and `paint-*` frames are the same side camera at about 3 m, with the car cropped at the bottom edge. References show a car filling the frame with detailed wheels, reflections of the environment, interior and lights. Racer shows a recognisable body shape and a spoked wheel but no environment reflection, no visible interior, and headlights that do not light the car's surroundings.
**Garage / UI (4 pairs).** References show a lit garage/showroom interior as the backdrop. Racer's garage screen is a text menu over a darkened street; there is no garage scene.

### Cross-category findings
1. The largest visible gap is world content (facades, signage, props, vegetation density), not post effects.
2. Lighting at night has no environment: lamps do not light walls or the road beyond the car, and windows/signs carry no detail.
3. Smoke needs shaping (wisps, lighting, ground contact), not just density.
4. Close-up presentation (camera, reflections, interior, garage backdrop) is missing, so the car itself cannot be compared fairly at reference scale.
5. Racer is far from "indistinguishable"; this round only enlarges the evidence base. It does not close any gap.

### Not established
- Whether any reference's look (grade, bloom, shadow filtering) is recoverable numerically from the raw games; the MCLA/MC3/GT4 comparisons used only names and authored values.
- Reference resolutions differ (640x480 to 1920x1080) and some carry compression and watermarks, so fine-detail comparisons are limited to what the sheets show at 480x270 per cell.

### Observed (round 2)
1. **Night is the widest gap.** Across the seven night sheets (55 pairs) the references have lit window rows, readable signs, and light blooms with star flares around lamps and headlights; the cars' lights light the road and nearby geometry. Racer's night frames show the same pink/violet flat walls, cyan/magenta blocks for neon, and a dark road lit mainly by a pale headlight cone. Tail lights are red geometry without bloom (`closeup-night-rear` against `mcla_033`, `j2hin_047`). Even the PC-only SLRR night frames (HUD overlays, low-poly) show lamp glow and headlight beams on the road that Racer lacks at street-level views.
2. **Scene variety is low.** Every Racer frame is the same intersection and the same three skyline buildings; the references vary location in every frame (Paris, Las Vegas, Hong Kong, Rome, London, Tokyo, Los Angeles). The fixed start position is a limit of this capture matrix, not only of the renderer.
3. **Day:** references have dense facades with signage, awnings, crowds, billboards and skyline depth. Racer's buildings are flat beige/grey boxes with no signage. Racer's sky and cloud lighting is the strongest element and is comparable to the PC-tier references (SLRR) but below the console ones (MCLA, GT4 Hong Kong/Paris).
4. **Artefacts that appear in Racer frames and in none of the references:** (a) a soft yellow ghost blob in the lower-left of nearly every day and dusk rear/front frame (the sun lens ghost, in a fixed screen position); (b) hard-edged dark rectangles with blue reflections on the road in `speed-day-6s/10s/14s` and every `v*-speed-day-10s` frame (the wet-road patches seen at speed); (c) at speed, bright dashes in the night sky (`speed-night-10s/14s`); (d) rear-braking smoke as a single opaque ball hiding the road and car.
5. **Smoke:** references show smoke attached to the tyres, thin, with lit particles and sparks (`mc3_038`, `j2hin_028`, `gt4_012`). Racer's side-view smoke is the nearest, but is one soft grey cloud behind the car; the rear view is the ball in (4d).
6. **Motion:** references (`mcla_012/027/035`, `gt4_065/067`, `mc3_015`) blur the surroundings, keep the car sharp and fill the frame with the car; Racer shows a tiny car with blur concentrated in the bottom third of the frame.
7. **Close-up:** all 17 car close-up and 4 wheel close-up references fill the frame with detailed wheels and body reflections; Racer's closest frame crops the car at the right and bottom edges, shows the wheel as a small grey disc and has no environment reflection. The four other cars look better lit than the NSX in the same frame, so the NSX paint is the weakest car in the set.
8. **Garage:** references show lit workshop, showroom, paint-booth and lift scenes (GT4 glass hall, Juiced 2 lift, SLRR garage and paint booth, MC3 workshops). Racer's garage is a text menu over a darkened street.
9. **Cockpit/dash:** references show interior, gear knob, wheel and gauges (MCLA, GT4, Juiced 2, SLRR). Racer has no cockpit or hood view, so those pairs only show the nearest rear view. This is a missing feature, not a rendering defect.
10. **Trees/vegetation:** references have varied dense foliage (maples, bamboo, pines); Racer's street trees are single leaf-card clusters that read as dark green clumps and show visible stair-stepping at the edges in several frames (`brake-day-left`, `speed-day-10s`).

### Inferred / unknown
- Inferred and then tested: 4(a) and 4(b) are defects of my own shaders. 4(a) turned out to be the sun/moon disc mirrored in the asphalt, not the lens ghost (see Artefact fix); 4(b) is the puddle mask.
- Inferred: items 1, 3 and 8 need authored or extracted assets (window textures, sign atlases, garage interior), not shader work alone.
- Unknown: whether the raw games' assets contain a recoverable facade/sign atlas that fits Racer's city mesh; the asset-mining notes record only names and authored values so far.
- Unknown: how much the compression and 480x270 cell size understate reference detail and Racer's fine artefacts.

### Artefact fix (same session, after the second round)
- **Cause established (observed):** the soft yellow disc (day/dusk) and pale disc (night) survive `--no-dof` and `--post-neutral`, so they come from the scene shader, not from DoF or the lens flare. A mirror image of the sun/moon at the lower-left of the road: `reflected=sky(reflect(-v,n))` returned the sharp sun/moon disc for asphalt (rough .95), and the Fresnel term is large at the grazing view angle.
- **Change (src/visual_shader.h):** `sky/daySky/nightSkyExtras` take a `disc` scale; the surface reflection passes `1-smoothstep(.25,.55,rough)` so rough surfaces no longer mirror the disc (the direct lobe still supplies the highlight); the direct Fresnel boost is roughness-limited (Lagarde form); the puddle mask edge is softer (`smoothstep(.64,.82)*.85` instead of `(.68,.74)*.9`).
- **Evidence:** `output/artefact-fix/disc-and-puddle-before-after.jpg` (before = round-1 matrix frames at 28e7412, after = working tree, three scenarios, same arguments and build otherwise). The disc is gone in all three; puddles are softer but still present at speed. `ctest` 96/96 (10 skipped for missing local assets); `visual_lighting_checks` now pins both guards and fails when the `disc` argument is removed (mutation-checked).
- **Not fixed:** puddles still read as flat dark patches with a blue tint at distance; the wet-road look is not at reference quality.

### Second fix batch (artefacts 4(c) and 4(d))
- **Night-sky dashes at speed:** the radial speed blur smeared stars into white dashes. The blur weight now fades out where the eye-distance target says "sky-far" (120-260 m; only sampled when the depth target exists). Evidence `output/artefact-fix/smoke-and-sky-dashes-before-after.jpg` (bottom row): dashes gone, stars remain as dots. `visual_lighting_checks` pins the expression (mutation-checked: weakening the factor fails it).
- **Rear braking smoke:** puffs now use a lumpy noise texture (three octaves of hashed value noise times the radial falloff), each puff is rotated by its seed, and tyre-smoke peak alpha dropped from .95 to .62. From behind it is still one cloud over the road (top two rows); side view is lumpier. Not at reference quality: no lit particles, sparks or tyre-attached wisps.
- The "before" frames are the frozen matrix build (before the disc fix too), so the disc is also gone in the after frames.

### Facade and sign textures (first asset-mining step from the comparison)
- **Source:** SLRR 2.2.1/2.3.1 ships 674 loose city textures in `games/raw/slrr-231/maps/textures` (Hungarian names: `ablak` window, `fal` wall, `kirakat` shop window, `haz` house, `iroda` office). A contact sheet of 96 of them (read) shows window rows, shop fronts with signage (`3_szurkehazalattikirakat`, `csapott_tover_szurkehaza*`, `Aszalon_nagykirakat`), neon-style sign sheets (`F_Logo`, `V_Logo`, `N_Logo`, `8_Logo`) and brick/siding/concrete walls. They load through Pillow's DDS reader (no codec written).
- **Leads checked for format work:** `awesome-game-file-format-reversing` (VelocityRa), `reshax.com` SLRR `.scx`/`.rpk` threads and 010 templates (`streetlegal_rpk.bt`, `streetlegal_rpk_mapmodels.bt`), `znxee/mc3-znx-tools` (MC3 `.pck`/`.bnd`), `TahmidAlam-git/MC3_extraction_tools`, `ImpossibleEchoes/RAGE-Console-Resource-Converter` (MCLA `.xtd`/`.xdr`, RAGE), `Ekey/JCED2.JFS.Tool` (Juiced 2 JFS archives, archived repo) and the `reshax` Juiced 2 `.d9m/.jsm/.zdf` thread. None of those was run here; only the loose SLRR DDS files were used. Whether the raw MCLA/MC3/Juiced 2 archives yield comparable facade textures through these tools is untested.
- **Change:** a new `RCRSURF3` atlas (256x2048, eight tiles: asphalt, pavers, stone, palm, siding `Hotel_fal`, storefront `3_szurkehazalattikirakat`, sign sheet `F_Logo`, concrete `beton79`), pinned by SHA-256 in `scripts/prepare_world_surfaces.py`, written to `assets/visual-local/v3` (local, gitignored). Reader accepts v1/v2 (4 tiles, padded grey) and v3. Buildings now use surface ids 3/8/10 for walls and 9 for the street-level storefront box (lit by its own texture at night); neon blades sample the sign tile (kept in the blade's hue, emission 2.4x at night instead of 6x so the artwork stays readable). The foliage test and shader domain were narrowed to ids 6-7 so ids 8-10 are not mistaken for palms; `world_scene_checks` asserts that every vertex above 7.5 is 8, 9 or 10.
- **Evidence:** `output/artefact-fix/facade-signs-before-after.jpg` (four scenarios, same arguments, before = frozen build with the 4-tile atlas). **Observed:** neon blades now show multi-coloured sign artwork instead of two flat colour blocks (clearest at night); the wall and storefront textures are hard to see in these 9 m frames because the walls are dark, far and heavily filtered. **Not achieved:** building facades still do not look textured at street distance, and there are still no readable signs on walls or lit window rows on the nearby buildings.
- **Tests:** `ctest` 96/96; `world_surfaces_checks` covers v3, mismatched magic/size and truncation; `prepare_world_surfaces_checks` builds a synthetic eight-tile atlas including resampled non-square tiles; `visual_lighting_checks` pins the atlas addressing and fails if the sign tile address reverts to the 1024-high form (mutation-checked).

### REA on the SLRR executable (this round)
- **Target:** `games/raw/slrr-231/StreetLegal_Redline.exe`, SHA-256 `f44fb88a4335c657e20701cdb84afb8d1d85f7c3aceb93d30113e6e6e44fefc2`, PE x86, Ghidra 12.2 through the REA MCP (`open_binary`, `search_strings`, `xrefs`, `procedure_pseudo_code`, `close_binary`).
- **Observed (strings):** the executable defines `.vsh/.psh` shader names `bump`, `bump_spec`, `mixtabumpspec_vcprelit`, `mixtaspec_vcprelit`, `mixtaspec_lightmapped`, `diff_envmap`, four `skinmesh*_diffenvmap`, `water_tss`, `blur.psh` and `clear_below.psh` (0x593ec0-0x5956d0), plus fog parameters `fog_start/end/enable/color` and lens-glow parameters `lf_glow_*` (0x5a865c-0x5a89b8). A search for window/emissive/self-illumination/neon terms found only Win32 window APIs and those fog/glow names, so no named "lit window" or "emissive" material was observed.
- **Observed (pseudocode, 0x43ea60):** a loader maps the shader name to an embedded blob (`mixtaspec_vcprelit.vsh` -> 0x594898, `mixtaspec_lightmapped.vsh` -> 0x594a18, skinned variants -> 0x594bb0-0x595308) and creates a vertex shader from it; any other name is assembled from `system\shaders\<name>` with `D3DXAssembleShaderFromFileA`.
- **Inferred:** SLRR world materials are vertex-colour pre-lit or lightmapped, so its night look and lit windows are baked into vertex colours/lightmaps rather than computed per frame. Racer's per-fragment lamp/neon lighting and hashed lit-window cells are therefore an authored substitute, not a port. Not examined: the pixel-shader blobs themselves, the lightmap texture format, and where the lightmaps are stored (likely in the `maps/*` meshes or `.rpk` resources).
- **Not done:** equivalent REA passes over MCLA/MC3/GT4/Juiced 2 for facade/sign data in this round (the earlier phase recorded only carrier string names for MCLA).
